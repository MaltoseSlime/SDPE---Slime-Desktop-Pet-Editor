This project separates two very different things under two different rules.
Read `LICENSE` for the full legal text of the engine license; this document
explains how the two pieces fit together in plain language.

  itself* for development/testing purposes.

Under MIT, anyone may use, copy, modify, merge, publish, distribute,
sublicense, and/or sell copies of the engine code, as long as the copyright
notice and license text are kept with any substantial portion of it.

### 2. Content made with the engine — All Rights Reserved by default

A "character package" (`.pet` file) or any other creative output a person
makes *using* this engine — artwork, sprites, character design, dialogue
writing, voice recordings, palettes, or any other creative asset — is **not**
covered by the MIT license above. It is an independent creative work, and:

- **Copyright belongs to its creator.** Using this open-source tool to make
  something does not put that something under MIT, and does not give the
  engine's authors, other users, or anyone else any rights to it.
- **The default is All Rights Reserved.** Unless a creator explicitly says
  otherwise (in the package itself, in a README, on the page where they
  share it, etc.), nobody else may copy, redistribute, modify, or reuse
  their character package or its contents.
- **Creators choose their own terms if they want to share.** A creator who
  wants to let others use, remix, or redistribute their `.pet` package is
  free to attach their own license or terms to it — for example a Creative
  Commons license (CC-BY, CC-BY-NC, CC-BY-SA, etc.), a custom EULA, or
  simple written permission. This project does not require or impose any
  particular content license; that choice belongs to each creator.
- **The engine does not grant content rights.** Being able to open, edit,
  or run someone else's `.pet` package in this engine is a technical
  capability, not a legal permission. Always check what the creator has
  actually said about reuse before redistributing or modifying their work.

### 3. Third-party components

This software embeds or depends on third-party open-source components
(notably the Godot Engine and the Blockly library), each under its own
license, separate from both of the above. See `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt`.
